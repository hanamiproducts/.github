<h1 align="center">Hanami Products</h1>

<p align="center"><em>Calm, focused tools, made one at a time.</em></p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://hanamiproducts.com"><strong>hanamiproducts.com</strong></a></p>

Hanami Products is a small, independent software studio. Each product does one job, does it quietly, and leaves the rest of your day alone. A one-person studio in Türkiye.

花見 *hanami*: the custom of stopping, for a moment, to look at the blossoms.

## Two in bloom.

### ✿ [Hanami Planner](https://hanamiplan.com) · hanamiplan.com

A planner made for ADHD brains. Break the big, vague task you keep putting off into small steps, as deep as you need. Put each step on one day, and see at a glance when a day is too full.

### ✿ [Hanami DNA](https://hanomidna.com) · hanomidna.com

G25 ancestry, explained plainly. Paste your G25 coordinates and see which ancient and modern reference populations sit closest to you. The results show genetic similarity, not documented family history.

<sub>Hanami Planner is a planning tool, not a medical product. Hanami DNA is not a medical or genealogy test.</sub>

## Open source

**[hanami_agent](https://github.com/samliumay/hanami_agent)**: a terminal AI agent, written from scratch in Rust. A learning project, at a very early stage. The product code in this organisation is private.
